PM Modi Inaugurates New Infrastructure Projects in Andhra Pradesh

The Prime Minister laid the foundation stone for several infrastructure projects in Andhra Pradesh, aimed at boosting connectivity, generating employment and driving regional development.

Andhra Pradesh, Oct 12: Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Saturday inaugurated and laid the foundation stone for a series of infrastructure projects in Andhra Pradesh, worth thousands of crores.

The projects include new roads, rail connectivity, industrial corridors and initiatives in the energy and port sectors.

Officials said the projects are expected to improve connectivity and support economic activity across several districts of the state.
